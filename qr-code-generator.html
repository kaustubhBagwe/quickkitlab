Free QR code generator for everyday sharing

Make a QR code for a website, plain text, email address, Wi-Fi network, phone number or WhatsApp chat. Customize its foreground and background colours, select an image size, and download the result as a PNG or JPG.

How to create a QR code

  1. Choose the type of QR code you want to create.
  2. Enter the website, message or contact details.
  3. Choose colours and image size, then generate your QR code.
  4. Scan-test the result and download the PNG or JPG file.

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. You can create and download QR codes without an account or subscription.

Can I create a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Select Wi-Fi, enter your network name and password, and choose the security type.

Is my QR content uploaded?

No. QR generation happens locally in your browser. The QR library is loaded from a CDN, but your content is not sent to QuickKitLab.
